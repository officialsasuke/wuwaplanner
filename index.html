Your resonators

Click a card to mark it as owned. Set the sequence (S0 to S6) and the signature weapon (R1 to R5, or none) with the arrows.

Import

Takes every resonator from your Convene history and sets the sequence from the number of copies (2 copies = S1). Values are only raised, never lowered. Weapons cannot be assigned automatically, set them on the cards.

Your weapons

Click a weapon to mark it as owned, then set the refinement (R1 to R5) and how many copies you have. Signature weapons set on the roster cards count too. Each team member gets the best of your weapons automatically: the small square on the team cards.

Import Convene history

  1. Start Wuthering Waves on your PC and open Convene > History. Wait until the list has loaded.
  2. Pick the game log. It is in your game folder under Client\Saved\Logs\Client.log. The planner reads the link from it, nothing is uploaded.
    or drop the file here
    If the file cannot be opened, close the game first. The link stays in the log.
  3. Or the old way: run this in PowerShell. It copies the link, then paste it below and click Load history.
With "Remember", Fetch history works next time without the log. The link gives read access to your Convene history while it is valid, so without "Remember" it is only kept until you close the page.

Privacy and backup

Your plan, roster and history stay in this browser only. The page talks directly to Kuro's official API, no other server is involved. Do not share your Convene link publicly. The script only reads your game log, you can read it yourself in import.ps1.
Clearing browser data deletes everything. Save a backup now and then, or to move to another PC or browser.

Import history

  1. Start Wuthering Waves and open Convene > History. Wait until the list has loaded.
  2. Click Search automatically. The planner reads the link from the game log and loads every banner.
  3. If that fails, enter your game folder or paste the Convene link.

Your own images

Put PNGs into the images folder next to the planner. The file name is the name, e.g. Hiyuki.png or Yangyang Xuanling.png. Upper and lower case, spaces and special characters do not matter. Portrait format (about 3:4) works best for characters.
